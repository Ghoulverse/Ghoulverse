# GOO RUNNER - GHOULVERSE

An epic endless runner where ghouls cleanse The Void of real bacteria, viruses, and filth!

**Play Now:** https://ghoulverse-game.vercel.app

## Game Features

### 8 Playable Ghouls
| Ghoul | Passive | Ultimate Ability |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| 👻 GOO | Double orb magnet | Ectoplasmic Overload - Screen wave |
| 👷 TRADIE | Piercing shots | Demolition Day - Massive damage all |
| 🧘 ZEN | Slows enemy bullets | Enlightenment - Invincibility + kill touch |
| 👩‍🌾 GARDEN | Health regen | Overgrowth - Vine trap |
| 🥳 PARTY | Random crits (20%) | Rave Mode - Rainbow explosions, 10x score |
| 💅 BEAUTY | Dodge = shield | Flawless - 360° beam sweep |
| 👨‍🎓 SCHOLAR | Reveals weaknesses | Knowledge Bomb - Weakness damage |
| 👶 BABY | Randomizes stats | Tantrum - Chaos mode! |

### 8 Real Enemy Types
- **E.Coli** - Gram-negative bacteria
- **Staph** - Biofilm-forming bacteria
- **Norovirus** - Highly contagious virus
- **Sludge** - Organic buildup
- **Black Mold** - Toxic fungal spores
- **Limescale** - Mineral deposits
- **Coronavirus** - Enveloped RNA virus
- **Biofilm** - Protected bacterial communities

### 4 Epic Bosses
- **MEGA E.COLI** - The mother of all bacteria
- **STAPH KING** - Crowned in golden slime
- **SLUDGE BEHEMOTH** - Ancient accumulation
- **MOLD EMPEROR** - Spore-lord of damp realms

### Tool System
Each ghoul has 3 unique tools to collect:
- Dual Nozzle, Wide Mist, Acid Rain (GOO)
- Hammer Smash, Jackhammer, Power Drill (TRADIE)
- Gong Wave, Lotus Shield, Meditation (ZEN)
- And more!

### Special Moves (Ultimates)
Fill your orb meter to 100, then press **SPACE** to activate your ghoul's ultimate ability!

### Educational Codex
Defeat enemies to unlock entries in the Codex - learn real science about bacteria and viruses!

### Daily Challenges
- Speed Demon - 2x speed, 3x score
- Purist - No tools, 2x score
- Glass Cannon - 1 HP, 5x damage
- Bullet Hell - 2x enemies
- And more!

### Audio
Procedural synthwave soundtrack that plays during gameplay. Toggle with the 🔊 button.

## Controls
- **Hold Click/Tap** - Rise up
- **Release** - Fall down
- **SPACE** - Activate Ultimate (when orb meter full)
